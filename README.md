# 🪫 ChargeFX_Website - Customize Your Charging Experience

Welcome to ChargeFX_Website, your gateway to a unique audio tool for customizing sound effects based on your battery levels.

## 🚀 Getting Started

ChargeFX is an easy-to-use website tool that interacts with your charging accessory. It allows you to play custom sound effects when your battery reaches specific percentage levels. Follow the steps below to get started with ChargeFX.

### 🌐 What You Need

To effectively use ChargeFX, you will need the following:

- A device that supports the NetM project charging accessory.
- The latest version of ChargeFX_Website.
- Basic computer access to download and run files.

## 📝 How to Use ChargeFX

Once you have downloaded and installed ChargeFX_Website, follow these steps to set it up:

### 1. Open ChargeFX_Website

Locate the downloaded file and double-click it to launch the application.

### 2. Connect Your Accessory

Ensure your NetM project charging accessory is connected to your device. The software will recognize it automatically.

### 3. Set Your Preferences

- Choose sound effects for different battery levels.
- Adjust the percentage levels at which each sound plays.
- Save your settings.

### 4. Test Your Setup

Once you have configured your preferences, plug in your device and observe how ChargeFX_Website interacts with the charging accessory.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

Below are some common issues users face and their solutions:

- **No Sound Notification:** Ensure your device’s audio is not muted. Check the volume settings on both the device and the accessory.
- **Accessory Not Recognized:** Disconnect and reconnect your accessory. Ensure it is properly working with other devices.
- **Software Crashes:** Restart your device and ensure you are using the latest version of ChargeFX_Website.
